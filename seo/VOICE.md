# VOICE.md

## Tone
- Calm, plain, neighborly
- Spelling: US English
- Sentence case for headings (matches the current design)

## Habits
- Answer first, explain after. Short sentences, plain words.
- "We" for the practice, "you" for the patient.
- Say what each step feels like; nervous patients are a core audience.

## Avoid
- Clinic-speak, buzzwords, "state of the art", "world-class"
- Claims without proof; promises of results

## Sample paragraph in our voice
Plenty of our patients arrive nervous. We listen first, tell you what each step will feel like, and stop whenever you need us to.
