This policy covers the Afia Dental website, afiadentalcare.com. It explains what information the site collects and how we use it. Your dental records are protected separately under HIPAA, as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

What this website collects

Browsing the website does not ask for your name, contact details or health information, and does not set cookies unless you open the booking window or the map.

  • Visit statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are useful. It does not use cookies and does not identify you. Cloudflare also hosts the site and processes standard request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and block attacks.
  • Online booking. The booking window is provided by Simplifeye, a dental scheduling company, and loads only when you click a booking button. The details you enter, such as your name, phone, email, insurance and reason for visit, go to Simplifeye and to our office so we can schedule you. When the booking window loads, Simplifeye also loads Google Analytics for our practice, which sets cookies (such as _ga) to measure how people use online booking. Simplifeye’s and Google’s privacy policies apply to those services. If you prefer, call us to book instead.
  • Map. The map on our Contact page comes from Google Maps and loads only when you tap “Show map.” Google may then set cookies and receive your IP address. Google’s privacy policy applies.
  • Phone calls. If you call us, we use what you tell us only to care for you and schedule your visit.

How we use information

We use booking information only to schedule and confirm appointments, check insurance and contact you about your care. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Children
