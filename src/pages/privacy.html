Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Afia Dental Care website, afiadentalcare.com. It explains what information the site collects and how we use it. Your dental records are protected separately under HIPAA, as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices, which is available at the office.

What this website collects

The website itself does not ask for your name, contact details or health information, and it does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Visit statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are useful. It does not use cookies and does not identify you. Cloudflare also hosts the site and processes standard request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and block attacks.
  • Online booking. When you book online, the scheduling window is provided by Simplifeye, a dental scheduling company. The details you enter, such as your name, phone, email, insurance and reason for visit, go to Simplifeye and to our office so we can schedule you. Simplifeye’s own privacy policy applies to that window, and it may store information in your browser to make booking work.
  • Phone calls. If you call us, we use what you tell us only to care for you and schedule your visit.

How we use information

We use booking information only to schedule and confirm appointments, check insurance and contact you about your care. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Children

Parents or guardians should book visits for children under 18.
