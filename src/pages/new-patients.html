Your first visit, step by step

  1. Book. Request a time online or call the front desk. Tell us whether it’s a routine visit or something that hurts.
  2. Confirm. We confirm your time, check your insurance and let you know what to bring.
  3. Arrive 15 minutes early. You’ll fill out your new patient forms at the front desk: contact details, health history and insurance.
  4. Meet your dentist. Full exam, X-rays, a professional cleaning and an oral cancer screening. We review your history and ask about your goals.
  5. Your plan. Your dentist walks you through any treatment you need and what it costs, with payment options if insurance doesn’t cover everything.

What to bring

  • A photo ID
  • Your dental insurance card, if you have one
  • A list of the medicines you take
  • Recent X-rays or records from your last dentist, if you have them
  • A parent or guardian for patients under 18

Insurance and payment

We accept most PPO dental plans. Benefits vary from plan to plan, so call (617) 325-4100 before your visit and we’ll confirm what yours covers.

No insurance? We offer flexible payment options and will build a treatment and payment plan that fits your budget. Your dentist always reviews the cost with you before treatment starts.
