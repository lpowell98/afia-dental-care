Cosmetic Dentistry in West Roxbury, MA

Want to love your smile again? We brighten, reshape and restore teeth with natural-looking results, from professional whitening and bonding to veneers, Invisalign® and all-ceramic crowns. Whether you want a small touch-up or a full makeover, we plan it around your goals.

When to consider cosmetic dentistry

Some patients want to feel confident for a wedding or graduation; others are simply ready for a smile they like every day. Cosmetic treatment can fix stains that home whitening won’t lift, close gaps, repair chips and worn enamel, and even out crooked or uneven teeth.

Cosmetic treatment options

  • Dental bonding. Tooth-colored resin repairs chips, cracks and small gaps. Quick and affordable.
  • Porcelain veneers. Thin custom shells over the front of your teeth improve shape, size and color.
  • Crowns. All-ceramic crowns strengthen damaged or worn teeth and look natural. See crowns and bridges.
  • Teeth whitening. Professional whitening in the office for fast results, or custom take-home trays.
  • Enamel shaping. Smooths uneven edges and small flaws, usually without numbing.
  • Gum reshaping. Balances a “gummy” smile by showing more of your natural teeth.
  • Invisalign®. Clear aligners to straighten teeth. See Invisalign.

Benefits beyond looks

A smile you’re happy with makes it easier to laugh, talk and have your picture taken. Many cosmetic treatments help function too: bonding can reduce sensitivity, crowns strengthen weak teeth, and straighter teeth are easier to keep clean.

Preparing for your consultation

Brush and floss as usual, and if you’re due for a cleaning, it’s best to do that first, since healthy teeth and gums are the right base for cosmetic work. Think about what you’d like to change. Some patients bring a photo of their younger smile; others just want to feel better in pictures. At your consultation, your dentist walks through the options, timelines and costs and answers your questions.

Frequently asked questions

How long do cosmetic treatments take?

Whitening can be done in one visit. Veneers take a few weeks, and Invisalign® takes months. We’ll tell you the timeline for your plan at your consultation.

Do cosmetic treatments hurt?

Most are comfortable and minimally invasive. We use gentle techniques and numbing when needed.

How long does professional whitening last?

Usually 6 months to 2 years, depending on habits. Brushing, flossing, limiting coffee, tea and red wine, and regular cleanings help it last.

What types of crowns do you offer?

Porcelain, ceramic, resin and metal. Porcelain and ceramic blend in best; metal is very durable for back teeth. We’ll recommend the best fit for your smile and budget.

What if I don’t have insurance?

We accept major credit cards and offer financing and payment plans for veneers, whitening, Invisalign and other treatments.

Do you offer cosmetic care for teens?

Yes. Teens often come in for Invisalign, bonding or whitening, for example before graduation.
