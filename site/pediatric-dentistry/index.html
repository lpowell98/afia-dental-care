Pediatric Dentistry in West Roxbury, MA

Your child’s smile deserves gentle, careful attention. Dr. Eriselda Bano makes every visit calm and positive, from the first baby tooth to the teen years, helping kids build healthy habits and treating problems early.

Why baby teeth matter

Baby teeth help children chew, speak and hold space for adult teeth. Regular visits catch small problems early, and simple habits that fit real family routines save time, money and stress later.

Your child’s first visit

We recommend the first visit by age one, or within six months of the first tooth. We keep it short and warm: a quick look around, a gentle exam, a simple cleaning if your child is ready, and a review of brushing, diet and fluoride. You leave with clear next steps and tips to use at home that day.

How we keep kids comfortable

We use the “tell, show, do” approach: we explain each step in kid-friendly words, show it, then do it, at your child’s pace. Parents stay involved and informed. If extra help is needed, nitrous oxide (laughing gas) gives mild relaxation that wears off quickly. For children with special needs, we can schedule quiet times, dim the lights or break care into smaller steps.

Preventive care and treatment

  • Cleanings, fluoride and sealants to strengthen enamel and protect back teeth from cavities.
  • Tooth-colored fillings placed gently with effective numbing.
  • Crowns and pulp therapy (a baby root canal) to save teeth with deep decay.
  • Space maintainers to hold room for adult teeth if a baby tooth is lost early.
  • Mouthguards for sports and wisdom-tooth monitoring for teens.

Brushing tips for home

Under age 3, use a rice-sized smear of fluoride toothpaste; ages 3 to 6, a pea-sized amount. Brush twice a day, floss once a day where teeth touch, limit sticky snacks and sugary drinks, and offer water between meals.

If your child has a dental emergency

For a toothache, chipped tooth or injury, call (617) 325-4100 right away. If a permanent tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown, put it back in the socket if you can or keep it in milk, and come in immediately. Don’t put a baby tooth back in; call us for instructions.

Frequently asked questions

When should my child first see a dentist?

By age one, or within six months of the first tooth coming in.

Do you see children for emergencies?

Yes. Call (617) 325-4100 for toothaches, chipped teeth or injuries and we’ll do our best to see your child the same day.

Do you accept insurance for kids’ visits?

Yes, we work with many insurance plans and offer flexible payment options for families without coverage.

Is the office easy to get to with kids?

Yes. The office is in the Ingleside Mall on VFW Parkway, with on-site parking and elevator access.
