Our dentists in West Roxbury

Five dentists who share one approach: listen first, explain clearly and keep you comfortable. Together they care for toddlers to grandparents in one office.

Dr. Alla Edriss, DMD

Owner and dentist · General and family dentistry

Dr. Edriss is the owner and primary dentist at Afia Dental. She leads the practice’s family care, from check-ups and cleanings to fillings and crowns, and makes sure every patient understands their options and costs before treatment begins.

  • Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD)

Dr. Raza Baig, DMD

General dentist · Restorative and cosmetic dentistry

Known for precision and kindness, Dr. Baig helps patients regain confidence with crowns, root canals, implants and cosmetic work. He is certified in Invisalign.

  • Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD), Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine
  • Fellowship in Advanced Periodontics, University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine
  • Bachelor of Dental Surgery, Riphah International University, Pakistan
  • Member, Massachusetts Dental Society and Connecticut State Dental Association

Dr. Eriselda Bano

Pediatric dentist

Dr. Bano brings warmth and patience to every child’s visit, so first trips to the dentist build trust instead of fear. Learn more about pediatric dentistry.

  • Boston University School of Dental Medicine, graduated with high honors in the top 10 of her class
  • Omicron Kappa Upsilon dental honor society
  • Pediatric residency, Tufts University School of Dental Medicine
  • Dental degree, University of Tirana Faculty of Dental Medicine

Dr. Suky Sidhu

Cosmetic dentistry · Smile makeovers, Invisalign and veneers

With over a decade in cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Sidhu focuses on natural-looking smile makeovers. His work includes Invisalign, veneers, crowns, bridges, composite bonding, white fillings and teeth whitening. His goal is a calm, comfortable visit and a plan that fits your needs and budget.

Dr. Natalie Sokoloff, DDS

General, restorative and preventive care

Dr. Sokoloff focuses on gentle, preventive care and uses the least invasive treatment possible so patients keep healthy smiles. She takes a whole-body approach, mindful of the link between oral health and overall well-being, and keeps up with continuing education to bring patients current care.

  • Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS), New York University College of Dentistry, 2008
  • About a decade at the MIT Dental Clinic in Cambridge
  • Years in private practice across Massachusetts
  • Sees patients Tuesdays and Fridays
