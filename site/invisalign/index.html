Invisalign in West Roxbury, MA

Invisalign® straightens teeth with a series of nearly invisible, removable trays instead of metal brackets and wires. It treats crowding, gaps and many bite problems, and fits into your daily routine.

How Invisalign® works

Treatment starts with a consultation. Dr. Raza Baig, who is certified in Invisalign, checks your teeth and talks through your goals. We take digital scans (no messy impressions) and build a 3D plan that shows how your teeth will move, step by step.

You wear each set of aligners 20–22 hours a day, taking them out only to eat, drink, brush and floss, and switch to a new set every one to two weeks. Most patients notice changes within a few weeks, and many finish in 6 to 12 months, depending on the case.

How your trays are made

Your scans and treatment plan go to the Invisalign lab, where your aligners are custom-made from a smooth, flexible material called SmartTrack and trimmed to your gumline for comfort. When they arrive, we check the fit and show you how to wear and clean them. Most patients start within a few weeks.

Benefits of Invisalign®

  • Discreet. The aligners are clear, so most people won’t notice them.
  • Removable. Eat what you like and brush and floss normally. No food rules, no threading floss around wires.
  • Comfortable. Smooth trays and no wire tightening.
  • Healthier teeth. Straight teeth are easier to clean, which lowers the risk of plaque, cavities and gum disease.

Check-ups during treatment

At your first fitting we may place small tooth-colored attachments on a few teeth. They help the aligners grip and guide your teeth, and they come off when treatment ends. After that you’ll visit about every 6 to 8 weeks so we can check progress and give you your next trays.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Invisalign cost in West Roxbury?

It depends on how complex your case is and how long treatment takes. You’ll get a clear estimate at your consultation, and we’ll review insurance and monthly payment options.

How long does Invisalign take?

Most cases take 6 to 12 months. Simple cases can be faster; complex ones take longer. We’ll give you a personal timeline at your first visit.

Can Invisalign fix overbites, gaps or crowded teeth?

Yes, for mild to moderate crowding, spacing and many bite problems. If another treatment would work better for you, we’ll tell you.

Do you treat teens with Invisalign?

Yes. Invisalign Teen includes features that help track wear time, which is useful for younger patients.

Can I take my aligners out to eat?

Yes. Take them out to eat and drink anything other than water, then brush and put them back in.

Will insurance cover Invisalign?

Many plans include partial orthodontic coverage that can apply to Invisalign. We’ll check your benefits, and we offer financing if you don’t have coverage.
