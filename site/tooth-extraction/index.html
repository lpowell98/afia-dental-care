Tooth extraction in West Roxbury, MA

When a tooth can’t be saved, we remove it gently and safely to relieve pain and protect the rest of your mouth. We always try to keep your natural teeth first, and when removal is the right choice, we make it as comfortable as possible.

When an extraction is needed

Common reasons include severe decay, advanced gum disease, a tooth broken beyond repair, crowding, infection and impacted wisdom teeth. We look at every option before recommending removal.

Simple and surgical extractions

  • Simple extractions are for teeth we can see and reach easily, often decayed or loose teeth.
  • Surgical extractions are for impacted wisdom teeth or teeth broken below the gumline, and involve a small incision.

What to expect

We start with an exam and digital X-rays, explain your options and fully numb the area. You may feel pressure, but no sharp pain. Sedation is available if you’re anxious. After the tooth is out, we clean the site, place stitches if needed and give you aftercare instructions.

Aftercare and recovery

Eat soft foods like yogurt, mashed potatoes and smoothies for the first few days, and avoid hard, crunchy or spicy food. Don’t smoke, spit forcefully or drink through a straw, since these can dislodge the blood clot and cause a painful dry socket. Most people feel much better within a week. If dry socket does happen, call us and we’ll treat it quickly.

Replacing the tooth

After healing, you can replace the tooth with a dental implant, a bridge or a partial denture. Implants are the most stable, bridges restore function quickly, and partials are an affordable option for several missing teeth.

Frequently asked questions

How long does recovery take?

Most simple extractions heal in 3–5 days. Surgical extractions, like wisdom teeth, can take 1–2 weeks. Following the aftercare instructions speeds healing.

Is a tooth extraction painful?

You won’t feel pain during the extraction because the area is numb, and sedation is available. Some soreness afterward is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.

Do you do emergency extractions?

Yes. For severe pain, swelling or infection, call (617) 325-4100 and we’ll do our best to see you the same day.

How much does an extraction cost?

It depends on whether it’s simple or surgical. You’ll get a clear estimate before treatment. We accept most insurance plans and offer payment plans.

Can I replace the tooth later?

Yes. Once you’ve healed, we’ll review implants, bridges and partial dentures with you.
