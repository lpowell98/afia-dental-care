Accessibility

Updated November 16, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use afiadentalcare.com and to book care with us. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we’ve done

  • The site works with a keyboard and screen readers, with a skip-to-content link and labeled controls.
  • Text and buttons meet contrast guidelines, and the site follows your device’s reduced-motion preference.
  • Pages work on phones and when zoomed to 200%.

Booking another way

The online booking window is provided by Simplifeye. If any part of it is hard to use, call us at (617) 325-4100 and we’ll book for you.

Our office

Our office is in Suite 307 of the Ingleside Mall at 1208B VFW Parkway. If you need help getting to the office or any accommodation during your visit, let us know when you book.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site doesn’t work for you, call (617) 325-4100, email info@afiadentalcare.com,. We’ll respond and fix it.
