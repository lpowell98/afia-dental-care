New patients

Your first visit is $139: a full exam, professional cleaning, 4 digital X-rays and an oral cancer screening.

Request an appointment

Insurance and payment

We accept most PPO dental plans. Benefits vary from plan to plan, so call (617) 325-4100 before your visit and we’ll confirm what yours covers.

Plans we work with

  • Blue Cross Blue Shield
  • Delta Dental
  • MetLife
  • Cigna
  • Altus Dental

Plan participation and benefits vary. Don’t see your plan? Call us; we may still be able to help.

No insurance? We offer flexible payment options and will build a treatment and payment plan that fits your budget. Your dentist always reviews the cost with you before treatment starts.

Your first visit, step by step

  1. Book. Request a time online or call the front desk. We’ll confirm your time and check your insurance.
  2. Arrive 15 minutes early. You’ll fill out your new patient forms at the front desk.
  3. Exam, cleaning and your plan. Your dentist walks you through any treatment you need and what it costs.

What to bring

  • A photo ID
  • Your dental insurance card, if you have one
  • A list of the medicines you take
  • Recent X-rays or records from your last dentist, if you have them
  • A parent or guardian for patients under 18
