Dental Check-ups in West Roxbury, MA

Regular check-ups are the foundation of a healthy smile. Whether it’s your first visit or your first in a while, a routine exam catches small problems before they grow, and a professional cleaning keeps your teeth and gums healthy between visits. New patients start with a $139 visit: exam, cleaning and 4 digital X-rays.

The exam

We start by talking about your dental history, medications, health conditions, allergies and any worries you have. Being open with us helps us care for you well. Then we examine your teeth, gums and bite for decay, sores, gum recession and gum pockets, which can be an early sign of gum disease. Every check-up includes an oral cancer screening.

The cleaning

Your hygienist removes plaque and tartar above and below the gumline, flosses between your teeth and polishes them smooth so plaque has a harder time sticking. Flossing may feel tender if you don’t floss often; that eases as you make it a habit.

Digital X-rays

New patients and anyone who hasn’t had X-rays in a while may need them. X-rays show what we can’t see, like decay between teeth, around the roots and below the gumline. Digital X-rays are quick and use a low dose of radiation.

Care between visits

  • Brush at least twice a day with fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss once a day
  • Use a fluoride mouthwash
  • Limit sugary foods and drinks, and avoid smoking

Frequently asked questions

How often should I come in?

Every six months for most people. Waiting longer gives plaque time to harden into tartar, which can lead to cavities and gum disease.

What does the $139 new-patient visit include?

A full exam, a professional cleaning and 4 digital X-rays.

What’s the difference between a check-up and a dental exam?

They overlap. A comprehensive exam, usually for new patients, also looks at the head and neck and gives us a complete picture of your oral health.

Why do I need X-rays?

They show decay, infection and bone problems below the gumline and between teeth that we can’t see in the mirror.

What if it’s been years since my last visit?

That’s common and nothing to be embarrassed about. We’ll start where you are and make a plan together.
