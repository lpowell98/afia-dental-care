Your dentist in West Roxbury

Afia Dental has cared for West Roxbury families for over 40 years. We’re in Suite 307 of the Ingleside Mall at 1208B VFW Parkway, near West Roxbury Crossings, and a short drive from Roslindale, Dedham, Needham and Hyde Park.

Whether you’re new to the neighborhood, overdue for a cleaning, in pain or thinking about changing your smile, we listen first and explain your options. Check-ups, fillings, same-day crowns, implants, Invisalign, kids’ visits and emergencies all happen in one office.

We accept most PPO dental plans, including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna and Altus Dental, and will help you check your benefits before treatment. New patients start with a $139 visit: exam, cleaning and 4 digital X‑rays.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

We accept most PPO dental plans. Coverage varies, so call (617) 325-4100 and we’ll confirm yours before your visit. No insurance? We offer flexible payment options and a payment plan that fits your budget.

Can you see me today for an emergency?

Usually, yes. For severe pain, a broken tooth or a lost crown, call us right away and we’ll do everything we can to see you the same day.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We see patients from toddlers to seniors, and Dr. Bano focuses on gentle, kid-friendly care.

Where do I park?

The Ingleside Mall has its own lot. Our office is Suite 307, at 1208B VFW Parkway, near West Roxbury Crossings.
