Do you accept my dental insurance?

We accept most major dental plans. Coverage varies, so call (617) 325-4100 and we’ll confirm yours before your visit.

What if I don’t have insurance?

We offer flexible payment options and will build a treatment and payment plan that fits your budget.

Can you see me today for an emergency?

Usually, yes. For severe pain, a broken tooth or a lost crown, call us right away and we’ll do everything we can to see you the same day.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We see patients from toddlers to seniors, and Dr. Bano focuses on gentle, kid-friendly care.

What happens at my first visit?

A full exam, X-rays if needed, a professional cleaning and an oral cancer screening. We’ll review your history and talk through any concerns.

What technology do you use?

Digital X-rays, intraoral cameras and same-day crown technology, plus Invisalign clear aligners.

How often should I come in?

Every six months for most people. Patients with gum disease or other conditions may need more frequent cleanings.

Where do I park?

The Ingleside Mall has its own lot. Our office is Suite 307, at 1208B VFW Parkway, near West Roxbury Crossings.
