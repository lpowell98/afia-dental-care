Emergency Dentist in West Roxbury, MA

When to call an emergency dentist

  • Severe or lasting pain that over-the-counter pain relievers don’t help
  • Swelling or an abscess, a sign of infection that needs prompt treatment
  • A broken, chipped, loose or knocked-out tooth
  • A lost filling or crown, which leaves the tooth open to infection
  • Bleeding that won’t stop

Minor tooth or gum soreness that lasts more than two days should also be checked.

How we treat dental emergencies

We start with a quick exam to find the cause, then relieve the pain and stabilize the problem.

  • Toothaches and infections: antibiotics if needed, and treatment such as a root canal or extraction to remove the source.
  • Broken or knocked-out teeth: repair with bonding or a crown, or replacement with an implant or bridge.
  • Lost fillings or crowns: replaced or repaired the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer same-day emergency care?

Yes. We set aside time every day for emergencies. Call (617) 325-4100 and we’ll do our best to see you today.

How much is an emergency visit?

The emergency exam is $59. Treatment costs depend on the problem, and you’ll get a clear estimate before we begin.

What should I do if a tooth gets knocked out?

Hold it by the crown, not the root, rinse it gently and keep it in milk or saliva. Call (617) 325-4100 and come in right away. Don’t put a child’s baby tooth back in; call us for instructions.

What if it happens when you’re closed?

We’re open Monday to Friday 9–5 and Saturday 9–3. Outside those hours, call us when we open. If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, or swelling spreading toward your eye or neck, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
